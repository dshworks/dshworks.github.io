dshworks

Community workshop for the DeepSeek Harness (dsh) ecosystem: verified guides, plugin discovery, skills, and dev tooling.

Not affiliated with DeepSeek. The official repo says it best: the harness is "an idea, an official showcase, and a source of inspiration". The ecosystem belongs to the community.

  • howto-dsh Verified field notes: traps, skills, hooks, profiles. Every claim dated and sourced so you can re-verify.
  • awesome-dsh-plugins Spam-filtered open-data registry of dsh plugins, bundles, and skills.
  • deepseek-harness Upstream: the official harness (npm @deepseek-ai/dsh). Developer preview; expect breaking changes.

Also in the org, private while under construction: handbook and dsh-workspace.

Shipping a plugin? Add the dsh-plugin topic to your repo and it gets picked up by the registry.
